Click a county to select or deselect it.

Hold down Q to select counties as you move over them.

Hold down W to deselect them.

Instructions

Move counties between states and explore how state boundaries and electoral totals change.

Choose a county (or several) to move to a new state. Then click the Move button and the state you want to move your counties to.

The current simulator recalculates electoral totals after county assignments change.
